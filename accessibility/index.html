Built to be usable

The site uses semantic sections, descriptive image alternatives, visible keyboard focus, accessible buttons, and native dialogs with keyboard dismissal. The mobile menu and product filters expose their current state. Every section remains visible without scroll-reveal effects.

Motion controls

The dimensional gallery responds to scrolling. Use “Pause 3D scroll” below it to stop that movement. Your device’s reduced-motion preference disables the scroll motion automatically. No hover animation, autoplaying video, or opacity reveal is used.

Keyboard and display

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and Escape to close a dialog. Use your browser’s zoom and text-size controls. Content adapts to phone and desktop widths.
