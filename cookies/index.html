What this site actually uses

The site code does not set browser cookies or load advertising, analytics, embedded social feeds, remote fonts, or tracking pixels. All product images, fonts supplied by your device, styles, and scripts are served locally. There is one optional local-storage preference used only if you ask the site to remember your privacy choice.

Allow or continue without saving

“Allow & remember” stores this preference. “Use without saving” removes any existing preference and closes the notice for the current page. Without storage, it can appear again on the next page or visit. Both choices give access to the same content. Neither enables analytics or advertising.

Change your mind

Open “Cookie settings” in the footer at any time and choose “Use without saving” to remove the stored preference. You can also remove localStorage in your browser’s site-data controls. Your browser’s private mode or storage restrictions may prevent the preference from being saved.

Hosting and external links

The eventual hosting provider may process technical request/security information and may use infrastructure cookies depending on its configuration. Private preview hosting may also use access/authentication storage controlled by that provider. The final public Vercel deployment must be checked for actual provider behaviour before launch. Clicking a connected WhatsApp or Instagram link would leave this site; those services have separate policies and may use their own cookies.

If the site changes

Adding analytics, advertising, embeds, a checkout, or a newsletter requires a new assessment of disclosures and consent. A notice cannot authorise undisclosed collection. No optional tracking scripts should run before the consent required for them. Read the Privacy policy for the wider information-handling draft.
