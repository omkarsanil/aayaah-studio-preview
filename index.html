Useful information

Ordering questions.

Practical details about quotations, custom work and delivery.

How do I find out the price?

Use “Request a quote” on a piece, or contact the studio with your idea. Price depends on the size, materials, detail and delivery destination. The full quote is agreed before an order.

Can I request a different size or colour?

Custom designs are part of the Aayaah brief. Send a reference, dimensions and preferred colours. Feasibility and the final specification are confirmed before work begins.

Can I buy directly on this website?

This is a showcase rather than an online checkout. Orders are discussed through WhatsApp or Instagram. Contact destinations are dummy placeholders during this preview.

Are these photographs of available pieces?

The product photographs are AI-generated concept placeholders, not Ali’s completed work or live inventory. The Aayaah logo is the original client asset. Approved product photography will replace the samples before launch.

What are the delivery and return terms?

Production time, delivery availability, charges and custom-order conditions are confirmed before payment. See the draft delivery policy and returns policy. Mandatory consumer rights are preserved.
