Shipping & delivery

Know the production time, delivery cost, and destination before you commit.

Delivery is agreed per piece

This preview does not promise live stock, worldwide shipping, free delivery, or a fixed lead time. Delivery destinations and carriers are to be confirmed by the owner. Before payment, the written quote should distinguish making time from dispatch and transit time and include the total delivery charge.

Your quote should include

  • The item, dimensions, materials, custom details, and approval process.
  • The expected dispatch date or a clear dispatch timeframe, plus estimated transit time.
  • The delivery address, supported destination, shipping charge, and any delivery restrictions.
  • Any taxes, customs or import charges and who is responsible, disclosed before payment.
  • Any collection arrangement, tracking availability, and handling instructions.

If a dispatch date changes

The seller should contact you before the promised dispatch deadline, explain the delay, propose a realistic revised date, and offer the cancellation and refund options required by law. Where U.S. mail/internet/telephone order rules apply, a supported shipping promise, delay consent, and prompt refund procedure are required. An unspecified lead time is not permission for indefinite delay.

Delivery problems

Check the delivery address in your order confirmation. Contact the seller if an item is lost, damaged, late, or marked delivered but not received. The seller remains responsible for the remedies required by applicable consumer law; customers will not simply be directed to a carrier in place of those rights.

Packaging and care

Artwork and clocks need appropriate protective packaging. Any wall fixing, battery specification, care directions, and installation requirements must be supplied for the actual product. Arrange safe, suitable installation and keep packaging until you have inspected the piece.

Returns address and disputes

The live business and returns address must be supplied before sales begin. Please use the contact page to check the current preview status. See Returns & cancellations for the proposed process. Mandatory legal rights remain unaffected.
