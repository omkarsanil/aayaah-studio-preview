The original Aayaah logo was provided by the client.

Quotes and acceptance

An enquiry is not an order. A written quote should state the complete price and currency, taxes and shipping, the specification, quote validity, payment schedule, dispatch timing, cancellation/return terms, and how acceptance occurs. A contract forms only when both parties accept the documented order under the applicable law. Do not make payment until these details and the seller’s identity are clear.

Custom designs and approvals

Agree the dimensions, colours, materials, wording, references, and the number and cost of design revisions before starting. Review spelling, dates, and design proofs carefully. Material changes after approval need a revised written agreement and any updated price or timing. Handmade variations should be described accurately and do not excuse defects or material differences from the agreed specification.

Payments

There is no payment gateway on this site. The seller must specify the verified payment method, deposit amount, balance due date, receipt process, and lawful cancellation treatment in the quote. Do not share card numbers or passwords in social messages. No unexpected charges or automatic subscription are authorised by using this website.

Your references and intellectual property

Only provide artwork, photographs, names, and designs you have permission to use. Existing third-party intellectual property remains with its owner. Ownership or licensing of a commissioned design must be agreed in writing; buying a physical piece does not automatically transfer copyright. Aayaah should obtain separate permission before using a customer’s personal details, private references, or finished personalised piece in promotion.

Using the site

Use the website lawfully. Do not attempt to disrupt it, misuse its contact channels, or copy protected brand material without permission. Reasonable access needs can be raised through the verified contact once available. External messaging providers apply their own terms and privacy practices.

Problems and responsibilities

Raise issues with the seller so they can be addressed using the agreed order details and applicable consumer protections. These terms do not exclude liability that cannot legally be excluded, mandatory guarantees, or your rights regarding faulty or misdescribed goods. No governing law, exclusive forum, forced arbitration, or broad liability waiver is inserted before the seller’s jurisdiction is known.

Related policies

Read Privacy, Cookies, Shipping, and Returns & cancellations alongside the final written quote. Policies may be updated prospectively, but an agreed order cannot be retrospectively changed without a lawful basis.
