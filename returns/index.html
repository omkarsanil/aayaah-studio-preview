Before placing an order

This is a preview: no products are sold and no payments are taken here. The following is a proposed store policy for owner approval. The final quote must identify the item, whether it is standard or personalised, and the return and cancellation terms before payment.

Standard, non-personalised items

Proposed voluntary policy: tell Aayaah within 14 calendar days of delivery if you wish to return a standard item, then send it back within 14 days of that notice. Handle it only as reasonably needed to inspect it and return it securely, preferably with its original protective packaging. Contact the verified seller first for the return address; do not send goods to the placeholder address.

For a simple change of mind, the customer pays return shipping only where this was disclosed before purchase and the law permits it. Any deduction for diminished value must be lawful, explained, and supported. Mandatory rights may give you a longer period or different conditions.

Custom and personalised work

Truly made-to-specification or clearly personalised goods may be excluded from change-of-mind returns where the applicable law permits. This exclusion must be explained before an order; it is not a blanket exclusion for everything handmade. Faulty, damaged, misdescribed, or non-conforming custom goods retain the protection required by law.

Cancelling a commission

Contact Aayaah as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel a commission. Before work starts, the proposed policy is a full refund. After work starts, any lawful deduction for work already done or committed materials must have been explained in advance, reflect reasonable actual costs, and be permitted by local law. A deposit is not automatically non-refundable. Statutory cancellation or refund rights override this proposal.

Damage, defects, or the wrong item

Contact the verified seller promptly with your order reference, an explanation, and photographs if reasonably available. Keeping packaging can help with a carrier claim. A short reporting request does not remove your statutory rights. The seller will arrange the remedy and return costs required by applicable law, which may include repair, replacement, price reduction, or refund.

Refunds

Refunds use the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise without extra cost. The proposed voluntary return processing time is within 14 days of receipt of the item or proof of return, where withholding until then is lawful. Shorter legal deadlines apply where required. Shipping refunds, including standard outbound delivery on a qualifying statutory withdrawal, are handled as the applicable law requires. Store credit will not be forced in place of a legally owed refund.

Your legal rights come first

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits mandatory consumer guarantees, rights for faulty goods, or applicable cancellation rights. For qualifying EU distance purchases, a 14-day withdrawal right may apply, with specific exceptions including clearly personalised goods. Local requirements must be confirmed for the seller and the countries served.
