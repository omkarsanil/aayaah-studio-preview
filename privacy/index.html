A small site, a clear approach

This draft describes the Aayaah showcase and the intended enquiry process. The website has no account system, checkout, mailing list, advertising pixels, analytics, embedded social feeds, or form submission endpoint. Typing an enquiry in the preview dialog does not send it to Aayaah.

Who is responsible?

Aayaah is the trading name shown on this site. The legal operator, business address, country, and privacy contact must be verified before the site accepts real enquiries. Placeholder contact: hello@aayaah.example. This address is not monitored and is not a working privacy request channel.

Information processed

  • Browsing: the hosting provider may process IP address, request time, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The final provider, locations, and retention must be confirmed at launch.
  • Privacy preference: if you choose “Allow & remember”, this browser stores one local preference with an expiry date, for up to 180 days. Choosing “Use without saving” removes it. See the cookie policy.
  • Enquiries after launch: if you choose to message Aayaah externally, the information you send may include your name, social profile, contact details, design reference, size, budget, and conversation history.
  • Orders after launch: an agreed order may require your delivery address and order/payment records. Do not send passwords or full payment card details through direct messages.

Purposes and legal grounds

Enquiry details are used to respond to requests and take steps toward an order. Order details are used to fulfil the contract, handle delivery and aftercare, and meet recordkeeping obligations. Where applicable, these activities rely on pre-contractual steps, performance of a contract, legal obligations, or a proportionate legitimate interest in security and resolving disputes. Optional marketing would require a separate choice and is not enabled.

Sharing and external services

Once real contacts are connected, WhatsApp and Instagram will process messages under their own terms. Delivery providers and payment providers may receive the details necessary for an agreed order. Aayaah does not add advertising trackers or sell enquiry data through this website. Third-party service processing, international transfers, and any necessary safeguards must be documented before launch.

Retention

This preview does not save enquiry drafts on a server. The local privacy preference expires after 180 days unless removed sooner. Proposed live practice: delete unconverted enquiries after 90 days from the last meaningful contact, unless needed for a dispute or requested follow-up. Keep order and tax records only for the period required by the seller’s jurisdiction; the exact period must be inserted before live trading. Message-platform retention is also subject to the provider’s controls.

Your choices and rights

You can clear browser storage, change your privacy choice using the footer, or avoid external messaging links. Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and complaint to a regulator. Consent can be withdrawn for future consent-based processing. Submit requests through the verified privacy contact once published; identity checks will be limited to what is necessary.

Children, tracking signals, and updates

This site is aimed at adults arranging art purchases and is not designed to collect children’s data. It contains no cross-site tracking or sale/sharing for targeted advertising; Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals do not change that setup. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and its date. Any future optional technology must be assessed before activation.
